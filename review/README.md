# External technical review v0.1

Start with the [Reviewer Guide](Scale-SETI_External_Reviewer_Guide_v0.1.pdf) and [Concept Paper](../concept-paper/Scale-SETI_Concept_Paper_Candidate_1.0.pdf). The [feedback form](Scale-SETI_Reviewer_Feedback_Form_v0.1.pdf) is optional; email comments are equally useful.

This folder also contains Word versions of the guide and form and an empty review log template. Outreach plans, invitation drafts, reviewer contact notes and correspondence are kept locally in `review/private/`, which is excluded from Git.

The standalone guide and feedback PDFs were chosen as canonical copies. Their extracted text matches the corresponding PDFs inside the review packet exactly, although the binary files differ. Original packet variants remain available in the local backup ZIP.

Copy the log template into `review/private/` before recording findings, reviewer identities or dispositions. Keep private responses and contact notes in `review/private/` (ignored by Git). The engineering material is organized under [phase1](../phase1/README.md). Acceptance gates and commissioning procedures remain in the design document; the standalone acceptance folder contains the supplied preregistration template, not newly invented acceptance records.
